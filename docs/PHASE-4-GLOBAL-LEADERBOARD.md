## Phase 4: Global Competitive Leaderboard

Introduce a global leaderboard that allows players to compete beyond their individual leagues and measure their skill against the entire player base.

### Opt-In Participation

Users can choose whether they want to appear on the global leaderboard through their Profile or Settings page.

Players who opt out:

* Continue playing matches normally.
* Continue receiving a hidden rating score.
* Do not appear in public rankings.

This prevents leaderboard abuse while still maintaining accurate player skill ratings.

### Rating-Based Ranking System

The global leaderboard will not be based on total wins, matches played, or accumulated points.

Instead, rankings will use a rating system inspired by Elo and FIDE chess ratings.

The amount of rating gained or lost after each match depends on the relative strength of both players:

* Defeating a higher-rated player awards a large rating increase.
* Defeating a lower-rated player awards a small rating increase.
* Losing to a lower-rated player results in a significant rating loss.
* Losing to a higher-rated player results in a smaller rating loss.

This approach ensures the leaderboard measures player skill rather than play volume.

### Benefits

* Rewards quality wins over quantity of matches.
* Prevents leaderboard inflation.
* Creates meaningful progression for highly skilled players.
* Encourages challenging opponents rather than farming weaker players.
* Produces a more accurate global ranking over time.

### Leaderboard Experience

Players can:

* View their global rank.
* View their current rating.
* Compare themselves against other players.
* Track movement up and down the leaderboard over time.

The leaderboard will continuously update as match results are recorded, ensuring rankings accurately reflect current player performance.
