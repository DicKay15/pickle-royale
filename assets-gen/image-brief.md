# Pickle Royale — Image Generation Brief

App context: "Pickle Royale" is a playful pickleball rankings web app for a
friend group. Visual identity: retro athletic-club poster meets arcade
scoreboard. Sticker-style art with thick outlines.

## Strict palette
- Cream paper: #FBF6EA
- Deep green ink (outlines): #142A1F
- Court green: #14604A
- Electric lime: #C9F73A
- Coral: #FF6B4A
- Gold: #FFC93C

## Style rules (all images)
- Flat vector-style illustration, NO gradients, NO photorealism
- Thick dark-green (#142A1F) outlines, like a die-cut sticker
- Chunky, friendly, confident shapes. Slight hand-drawn warmth.
- No text in the images.

## Image 1 — App icon
**Save to:** `assets-gen/icon-512.png` (1024x1024 or 512x512, PNG)
A cheeky pickleball character: a round lime-green (#C9F73A) wiffle ball with
a few darker circular holes, a confident smirk, wearing a slightly tilted
gold crown (#FFC93C). Centered on a solid court-green (#14604A) rounded
background that fills the whole canvas edge-to-edge. The ball should fill
~70% of the frame. Bold, readable at small sizes.

## Image 2 — Hero mascot
**Save to:** `assets-gen/mascot.png` (1024x1024, PNG, transparent background
preferred; if not possible use solid #FBF6EA cream)
The same crowned pickleball character, full body this time: stubby arms and
legs, holding a pickleball paddle (court-green face, gold edge) over its
shoulder like a champion, mid-strut, little motion lines. Dynamic, joyful,
victorious energy.

## Image 3 — Empty court illustration
**Save to:** `assets-gen/empty-court.png` (1024x768-ish, PNG, transparent or
cream #FBF6EA background)
A tiny top-down or 3/4 view pickleball court (court-green surface, cream
line markings) with a single lime wiffle ball resting in the middle and a
paddle leaning against the net. Quiet "before the storm" mood. Minimal,
lots of negative space.
